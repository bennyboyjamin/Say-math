Say Math Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

The short version: Say Math does not collect, send, sell or share any personal information. Everything stays on your device.

What the app stores

Say Math saves game progress on the device it is played on: player nicknames, chosen grade, pet, coins, stars, practice history and settings. This lets kids pick up where they left off.

This information never leaves the device. We have no servers, no accounts and no way to see it.

What the app does not do

  • No sign-up, login, email address or real names required. Players use a nickname.
  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or third-party code that collects data.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos access.
  • No in-app purchases, chat or links to other websites.

Read-aloud

When "read aloud" is on, problems are spoken using the device's built-in voice. This happens on the device and nothing is sent anywhere.

Children's privacy

Say Math is made for children and is designed to follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws by not collecting personal information at all.

Parents' controls

The grown-ups area lets a parent see progress, change settings, make a backup code, or erase all saved progress. Deleting the app also deletes all of its saved information.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions? Use the support contact listed on the Say Math page in the App Store.
